Build a SaaS application for managing a small company's employees,
projects and tasks.

Employees can belong to departments.

Managers can create projects and assign employees to projects.

Projects contain tasks.

Tasks have statuses such as todo, in progress and completed.

Employees should have dashboards showing their assigned tasks.

Managers should have dashboards showing project progress.

Users must log in.

Managers can create, edit and delete projects.

Employees can update their assigned tasks.
